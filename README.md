# The Epsilon Archive

A curated problem archive for students preparing for **quant trading interviews** and the **Putnam Competition** with editorial commentary on problem-solving strategy.

Every problem here is chosen because it trains a transferable mental move. The solution matters less than the insight behind it.

---


## What makes this different

Most problem sets give you problems and solutions. This archive gives you:
- The **key insight**: the one explanation that makes the problem unfold
- The **trap**: what most people try first and why it fails
- The **category**: what concept this problem is actually training

## Difficulty Scale

- **P1** — Accessible with AMC/probability basics. Core insight is clear.
- **P2** — Requires combining two ideas. Some algebra or casework.
- **P3** — Putnam / interview difficulty. Non-obvious setup required.
