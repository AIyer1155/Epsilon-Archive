THE EDITORIAL FORMAT

Every problem, four lenses.

Getting the right answer isn't the point. Understanding the structure of the problem — and what it's actually testing — is. Each entry in Epsilon Archive is written to expose the machinery beneath the math.

I

The Trap

What the problem wants you to think, and why that path fails. Named explicitly so you can recognize it next time.

II

The Insight

The reframe that unlocks the problem. Often one clean observation that makes the rest mechanical.

III

The Solution

Rigorous but readable. Written for someone who wants to understand, not just verify.

IV

What This Trains

The transferable skill. Connects this problem to the broader pattern it belongs to — in Putnam, in quant interviews, in research.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Built for the gap no resource fills.

Most quant prep resources give you problems and solutions. A few give you hints. Almost none tell you what cognitive mistake the problem is designed to elicit — or what transferable skill solving it correctly builds.

Epsilon Archive was built to fill that gap. It's not a problem dump. It's a curated, editorially rigorous archive aimed at students who want to understand how they think, not just practice until they get lucky.
